About Us

Hidden Gem in Karatina

Aberdare Gardens & Resort is a tranquil haven located in Karatina, Nyeri County, known for panoramic ambience, lush landscaped gardens, and serene river views. We offer modern accommodation, exquisite dining, recreation, and event hosting.

Location & Accessibility: Address: 162 Karatina Gaikuyu Gitunduti Road, Karatina, Kenya. Located about 30 km from the Baden-Powell Museum and roughly a 1.5-hour drive from Nanyuki Airport. Pet-Friendly: The property is noted for being pet-friendly, allowing cats and dogs free of charge.

River Views Pool Camping Weddings Events Family-Friendly Free Wi-Fi Parking 24/7 Reception Pet-Friendly

Our Services

  • Lush gardens with expansive manicured lawns perfect for events, photography & nature walks
  • Large crystal-clear outdoor swimming pool with a relaxing poolside breeze lounge
  • 52 elegant rooms & family cottages with private balconies, garden views & Smart TVs
  • On-site restaurant serving diverse farm-to-table cuisine with an active bar & lounge
  • Kid-friendly outdoor play areas featuring a trampoline & family entertainment spaces
  • Modern conference rooms & meeting packages for corporate events and functions

Kitchen Hours: 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM
