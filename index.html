About Us

Hidden Gem in Karatina

Aberdare Gardens & Resort is a tranquil haven located in Karatina, Nyeri County, known for panoramic ambience, lush landscaped gardens, and serene river views. We offer modern accommodation, exquisite dining, recreation, and event hosting.

Location & Accessibility: Address: 162 Karatina Gaikuyu Gitunduti Road, Karatina, Kenya. Located about 30 km from the Baden-Powell Museum and roughly a 1.5-hour drive from Nanyuki Airport. Pet-Friendly: The property is noted for being pet-friendly, allowing cats and dogs free of charge.
